## The scenario: when we're a hard sell

| Signal | FirstCape |
|---|---|
| Buyer is a senior technologist who tests every claim | Transformation lead, ex-CTO Jarden, ex-CIO Westpac NZ (A) |
| Incumbents cover most of what we'd pitch | Microsoft 365 group-wide, Dynamics at Consilium (DNS, internal only), FNZ core platform (May 2026), InvestCloud adviser front end with onboarding phases planned (Oct 2024) |
| He said no to the usual next step | "Don't do any further work. If it looks interesting to me, then I'll give you a shout." |

## The strategy we took

| # | Move | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Respect the "no further work".** No first-meeting deck, no light config, no demo build; send only what he asked for (a written comparison) | Building against an explicit no burns hours (Navalo lesson) and costs credibility |
| 2 | **Research before arguing.** Public news + passive tech signals (page source, public DNS: kept internal, never told to the customer) | Found what kills the obvious pitch: onboarding is already contracted (FNZ + InvestCloud), Dynamics present, Claude in use |
| 3 | **Move off his example, and say why.** He picked onboarding; we used "one client across JBWere, Harbour, Consilium" (the step before account opening), and called it one example | Competing with two signed vendors on their own scope loses; explaining the move shows we listened |
| 4 | **Concede what the incumbent can do.** "Both can build it. The difference is how." Compare slide = same goal, two routes, never "they can't" | Renzo's read: "single customer view" alone gives no reason to choose Creatio over Dynamics |
| 5 | **Show what we think he runs, with a source per item, and ask to be corrected** | A technologist trusts sourced homework; corrections are discovery |
| 6 | **One qualifying question, not a meeting ask:** "What are your JBWere advisers using for client relationships?" | Dynamics everywhere → narrow to an AI Studio use case or step back |
| 7 | **Answer his buying questions fast and exactly** (Unlimited vs Enterprise seat break-even; running in their own tenancy) | His most buying-like questions on the call |
| 8 | **Customer-safe hand-off:** PDF only (speaker notes were internal), not the AE's intro deck (Bank.ai branding he criticised, unchecked claims) | Notes mentioned the DNS research and our tactics |

Offer held back for a reply: a timed build of the journey he names, at our cost, against a Dynamics estimate.
