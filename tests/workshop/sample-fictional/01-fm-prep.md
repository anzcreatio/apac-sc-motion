## How it works today (what we heard)
- Jobs come in from client emails and a client portal that some big clients use. A dispatcher keys each one into **JobTrak**, an old in-house job system, plus a few spreadsheets "to fill the gaps". **C**
- Technicians get jobs by phone call or SMS from the dispatcher. No mobile app. **C**
- Invoices are raised in **MYOB Advanced**. **C**
- Main frustrations: SLA breaches (clients are starting to apply penalties) and slow invoicing after jobs are done. **C**
- JobTrak was built by a contractor about 12 years ago; nobody wants to keep it. **I**
