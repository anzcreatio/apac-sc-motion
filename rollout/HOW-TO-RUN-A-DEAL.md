# How to run a deal with Claude

One page. If you only remember one line: **start each step in a new chat with "what's next on <account>"**. Claude
reads the deal folder and tells you the one next step, the skill and what it needs from you.

## 1. The deal, step by step

| When | Start a new chat and say (copy, fill in the <>) | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **New deal** | `New deal: <account>. Here's the <BDR / AE> call transcript: <paste or file>. The AE is <name>; the meeting is <date>.` (Or post it in the **APAC Transcript Submission** channel in Teams: it's picked up within 30 minutes on weekdays.) | Claude asks "introduction or detailed demo?", then the prep, the standard deck and the meeting plan, starting from what we learnt on similar deals |
| **Before the meeting** | `what's next on <account>` | Deck checks, the AE brief, anything still open |
| **Before any build** | `Plan the demo for <account>` | The three pre-build questions (competition, anything from the customer, anything else), then a visual build plan. **Nothing is built until you approve it** |
| **Build** | `Build the approved plan for <account> on stand <stand>` (a chat of its own, in your usual deals folder) | Builds without stopping, apart from the six hard stops, then a build report |
| **After every meeting** (same or next day) | `Meeting review for <account>: here's the transcript. I presented <deck file>.` | What landed, what to change, better answers to their questions, commitments for your desk, the next session |
| **Within 2 days of a demo** | `Harvest <account>` | The demo saved to the library, so the next deal reuses it |
| **RFP or workshop** | `RFP for <account>: <files>` · `Plan a workshop for <account>` | Fit-gap and bid / no-bid · pre-work, agenda, then the playback |

Any time:

| Say | For |
|---|---|
| `what's on today` | Your top 5, today's meetings and what's not ready, follow-up drafts (also every weekday at 8 am if you set it up) |
| `done: <what>` · `add: <what> for <account>, <owner>, <date>` | Keeping your commitments current |
| `how do I explain <X>?` | A one-liner, a 30-second answer, what not to say |
| `coach me on this call: <transcript>` | Private coaching, kept on your PC; you choose what to share |
| `/sc-lessons` (a chat of its own) | Anything you learnt: a product limit, a question we should have asked, an R&D answer |
| `feedback: <what happened>` | Something in a skill didn't work or could be better |

## 2. New chat or same chat?

| Start a **new chat** when | Stay in the **same chat** when |
|---|---|
| You start a new step (prep, review, plan, build, harvest, RFP, workshop) | You're fixing or redoing what this step just made ("shorter", "swap slide 4", "rebuild the deck") |
| You switch to another deal | You're answering Claude's questions for this step |
| The chat is long, Claude slows down, or says it summarised the conversation | You're closing a commitment or asking what's next right after the step |
| You've just updated the plugin | |

Everything that matters is saved in the deal folder, so a new chat loses nothing. Start chats in any folder; for
builds, use the folder where your build permissions are set (`sc-setup` sets this up).

## 3. Ten habits that win deals

1. **Ask the demo depth first:** an introduction to Creatio, or a detailed demo on their pains?
2. **Agree the closing ask and the next step with the AE the day before.** Same words for both of you.
3. **Book the next meeting before you leave the call**, and put the date on the next-steps slide.
4. **Answer the three pre-build questions and approve the build plan** before anyone touches a stand.
5. **Rehearse on the device, network and login you'll present with.** Have a fallback ready.
6. **Review every meeting within a day**, with the transcript and the deck you actually presented. Everything else
   (commitments, lessons, the next deck) hangs off the review.
7. **Pick the commitments** Claude captures after the review, and give each one a date.
8. **Harvest within 2 working days** of a demo.
9. **Nothing reaches the customer without your SC check.** Claude never sends anything.
10. **Claims only from the catalogue**; AI, data and hosting only from the "can say" lines. Otherwise: "we'll
    confirm in writing".

## 4. Good to know

- **Your desk is private:** your brief and workload stay on your PC; nobody else sees them. The desk tracks our
  tasks, not the pipeline: stage, forecast and value stay in the AE's CRM.
- **Coaching is for you, not about you.** It stays on your PC unless you share it.
- **Feedback about the skills is for everyone**, so tell Claude when something is wrong; Renzo reviews it on Fridays.

Questions: ask Renzo.
