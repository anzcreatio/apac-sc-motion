[00:00:12] **Priya:** Thanks for making the time, Greg. Can you tell me a bit about Kestrel and your role?

[00:00:20] **Greg:** Sure. We do commercial facilities maintenance: HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fire services,

[00:01:05] **Priya:** What prompted you to take the call?

[00:02:30] **Priya:** How do jobs come in today?

[00:02:35] **Greg:** Mostly email into a shared mailbox, maybe 60 percent. A few big clients have their own portals,

then there are spreadsheets for the SLA times per contract and who's inducted on which site.

[00:04:10] **Priya:** And getting the job to a technician?

[00:04:14] **Greg:** Phone call or SMS from the dispatcher. No app. The tech rings back when they're done, or doesn't.

[00:05:02] **Priya:** What does it cost you when it goes wrong?

[00:05:06] **Greg:** The abatements, and slow invoicing. A job finishes, the job sheet comes back on paper or a photo,
and billing rewrites the tech's notes into invoice lines. Average is about three weeks from complete to invoice.
I'd love that to be days.

[00:07:40] **Priya:** Who else would be involved in choosing a new system?

[00:07:45] **Greg:** Me, Shannon who leads dispatch, and our finance lead for the billing side. IT will want a say

[00:09:20] **Priya:** Are you looking at anyone else?

[00:10:15] **Priya:** Timeline?

whoever we pick.

[00:11:30] **Priya:** Budget?

[00:11:33] **Greg:** Not set yet. Depends on what the business case says.
