# Pre-work email (the AE sends it, a week before)

Write it in the AE's voice, short, per `../../standards/WRITING-STYLE-v0.1.md`. Fill the brackets; drop any line
that doesn't apply.

---

**Subject:** <Journey> workshop on <date>: a few things to bring

Hi <name>,

Thanks for making time on <date>, <time> (2 hours, <place or Teams>). We'll spend it on one thing: how
<journey, their words> runs today, what gets in the way, and what would make the biggest difference.

To make the most of the 2 hours, could you send these by <date, 2 working days before>? Rough is fine.

1. The spreadsheets, forms or reports your team uses for <journey> today (copies with the names changed are perfect).
2. The systems involved, in a line each (for example "SAP for invoices, Outlook for bookings").
3. Who does what: the roles involved and roughly how many people in each.
4. Rough monthly volumes (for example jobs, applications, enquiries).

It'd be great to have in the room:
- someone who does the work every day,
- their manager,
- whoever owns the result (<the outcome, their words>),
- someone from IT if they're free (optional).

We'll bring a first draft of the journey, based on what you've told us so far, for you to pull apart. We'd like to
record the session so we can focus on the conversation rather than notes. We'll check that's OK on the day.

Thanks,
<AE>
