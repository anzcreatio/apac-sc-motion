# Workshop agenda: 2 hours, one journey

SC facilitates · AE scribes on the capture sheet · Teams records (say so at the start and ask if that's OK).
The draft journey is on screen the whole time; their own spreadsheet or form beside it.

| Min | Block | What the SC does | What we leave with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | **Why we're here** | Introductions by role. "By the end we want your journey written down the way it really runs, and the three things that would make the biggest difference." Agree the journey and the persona on the draft | Scope in their words |
| 10–25 | **Outcomes** | "If this worked, what number moves?" Three measures. For each: today's figure, the target, who owns it. A guess is fine; mark it and ask for the real figure later | Home page KPIs, success criteria (`outcomes`) |
| 25–80 | **Journey walk** | Step by step on the draft. Ask "what's wrong with this?" For each step, the five prompts on the capture sheet: who, the thing and its status, the system or channel, the rule or approval and its number, the pain and what it costs. Open their spreadsheet: its columns are the fields | Stages, roles, rules, pains, requirements, channels |
| 80–95 | **AI moments** | "Where does someone read, summarise, type up or decide the same thing every day?" For each: who must check it, what data it touches | `ai_moments` |
| 95–108 | **Playback in the room** | Read back the top 5 pains and the musts. "Is anything missing or wrong?" Then a quick vote: each person picks their top 3 | C tags; `mandatory` on the musts |
| 108–120 | **Next steps** | Date for the next workshop ("we'll open it on this journey, built"). What they send and by when (example data with the details changed, real figures, an IT contact). Who signs off the playback | Commitments with names and dates |

## Time rules

- The journey walk absorbs overruns. If it runs long, AI moments move to the follow-up email.
- **Never cut the playback or the next steps.** Without them the workshop has no C tags and no next date.
- Integration detail is not for the room: note the system, the direction and move on. The IT follow-up takes it.
- A debate about a rule (a limit, an SLA, who approves): get both views and the number, mark it **disputed**, move on.
  It becomes the live change in the next session.
