# B5. Your brand and mobile

**Track 1 · 60 min · your development environment · Uses:** the toolkit's branding and mobile page conversion

## Why it matters

People adopt what looks like theirs and works where they are. The toolkit can apply your brand (colours, fonts,
logos, favicon) and turn a web page into a page for the Creatio Mobile app, so inspectors can work on site.

## Lab (practice scenario)

1. **Brand:** ask Claude to brand the app from a colour palette or your company website. It walks you through the
   palette and asks for logos. Apply it to your development environment only.
2. **Look at it:** open the app in your browser and check contrast and readability (the toolkit checks too).
3. **Mobile:** ask Claude to make the Inspection page available on mobile. It converts the web page into a mobile page.
4. **Try it:** open the Creatio Mobile app on your phone, connected to your development environment, and open the hero
   inspection. Add a finding from the phone.

## Check

- [ ] Your colours and logo show in the development environment.
- [ ] The Inspection page opens in the Creatio Mobile app, and a finding added there shows on the web.

## Watch out for

- **Branding affects everyone using that environment.** Agree it with whoever else uses it.
- **Branding mixed with business changes in one request:** keep branding requests pure; the toolkit treats a mixed
  request as app work with a plan.
- **Mobile is a separate page:** later web changes need converting again.

## For the facilitator

- Bring a sample logo file in case they don't have one to hand.
