# B6. Capstone: your own app, end to end

**Track 1 · 120 min · your development environment · Uses:** everything from B0-B5

## Why it matters

The point of the course is that you can do this on your own, for your own business. In the capstone you take a
small, real use case from idea to a working first version, then hand it to IT to move through test to production.

## Lab (your own use case)

1. **Pick something small** that people would use next month: a request log, an approval, a register your team
   keeps in a spreadsheet. One page of scope.
2. **Business Plan:** ask for it, answer the questions, fix what's wrong, approve it (B1).
3. **Build it** in your package (B2-B3), extending CRM objects where it touches clients or contacts (B4).
4. **Test it like a user** with practice records: every status, every rule.
5. **Write the hand-over** for IT in five lines: what it does, the package it's in, what it changes on existing CRM
   pages, how you tested it, who owns it.
6. **Show it** to the group in five minutes.

## Check

- [ ] An approved Business Plan, and the app built from it.
- [ ] Every rule and status tested with practice records.
- [ ] The five-line hand-over exists, with the package named.

## Watch out for

- **Scope creep:** a first version people use beats a big one nobody finishes.
- **Real customer data in a development environment:** use practice records unless your data policy allows otherwise.

## For the facilitator

- Help them cut scope in step 1. If the use case needs integrations or code, steer them to a smaller first version.
- Give their hand-over to whoever runs Track 2's deployment (D2).
