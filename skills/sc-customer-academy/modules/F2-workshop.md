# Workshop: build on Creatio with Claude (up to 3 hours)

**Up to 3 hours · your test site · you'll leave with two apps: the practice one and your own idea**

| Time | Part |
|---|---|
| 0:00-1:00 | **Part 1. Your first app** (the one-hour session: rules, ask, build, change your CRM) |
| 1:00-1:10 | Break |
| 1:10-2:10 | **Part 2. Your own idea** |
| 2:10-2:40 | **Part 3. Make it yours** (pick one: phone, look, or a rule) |
| 2:40-3:00 | **Part 4. Getting it live, properly**, and what's next |

Short on time? Drop Part 3 and finish in 2.5 hours.

## Part 1. Your first app (60 min)

The one-hour session ("Your first Creatio app in an hour"), from "The three rules" to "Change your CRM".
Skip its wrap-up: Part 4 covers it.

## Part 2. Your own idea (60 min)

1. **Pick something small** your team would use next month: a request log, an approval, a list you keep in a
   spreadsheet today. If you can't describe it in three sentences, make it smaller. (5 min)
2. **Ask for it** in three sentences: who uses it, what they record, and what happens to it. Answer Claude's
   questions. (15 min)
3. **Read the plan** before you say yes. Check: it reuses what's already in your CRM (clients, contacts) where it
   should; the dropdowns have the right values; nothing you didn't ask for. Change what's wrong, then **yes, build it**. (10 min)
4. **Try it** like a user, with made-up records. Ask for two changes. (25 min)
5. **One sentence** to the group: what you built and who would use it. (5 min)

## Part 3. Make it yours (30 min, pick one)

**On the phone:**

> Make the inspection page available on the Creatio mobile app.

Then open the Creatio Mobile app on your phone, connected to your test site, and add an inspection.

**Your look:**

> Brand this app with our colours from our website, <your website>.

(This changes the look for everyone on your test site; agree it with them first.)

**A rule:**

> On the inspection page, make Notes required when the result is Fail.

Test it: save a Fail without notes.

## Part 4. Getting it live, properly (20 min, talk, no typing)

Everything you built today sits in **your own folder of changes** (a Creatio package) on your **test site**. Getting
it to your live site is your IT team's job, and it's a simple routine:

1. Someone other than the builder **looks at the change**.
2. It goes to a **test site** first and gets tried like a user would.
3. Then IT **moves it to live** at a time that suits the business, with a way to undo it.

Agree with your IT lead today who does each step. Want to learn that routine hands-on? That's the IT track of the
full course.

## Check (the facilitator looks with you)

- [ ] The practice app works and your client page shows its tab (Part 1).
- [ ] Your own app was built from a plan you read and approved (Part 2).
- [ ] One "make it yours" change works (Part 3).
- [ ] You know who in your team does the three "getting it live" steps (Part 4).

## For the facilitator

- **Rehearse Part 1 on their product and site** and time the build; have your pre-built copy ready.
- In Part 2, help people cut scope in the first 5 minutes; that decides whether they finish. Steer anything with
  integrations or code to "version two".
- Bring their IT lead to Part 4 if you can. Data, hosting and AI questions: answer only from the governance pack's
  "can say" lines; otherwise write it down and confirm in writing through the AE.
- Groups over 6: pairs. Two facilitators for more than 12.
