# Pattern book

Good ideas for demo configuration that any SC can use and add to. **Ideas, not rules:** `sc-demo-plan` suggests
them; the SC decides. The floor every demo meets is the demo bar (`../standards/DEMO-BAR-v0.2.md`), not this.

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `catalogue.json` (this repo) | Every pattern: what, why it works, pains it fits, native/custom/AI, how to build it in Creatio, hours, source, where it's proven |
| SharePoint `Patterns/<deal or source>/` | Screenshots |
| SharePoint `Patterns/_inbox/` | **Drop new ideas here**: a screenshot, a link, two lines on why it's good |

## Adding a pattern

Anyone drops it in `Patterns/_inbox` (a competitor demo screenshot, a Knowledge Hub page, something from your own
build). `sc-inbox` picks it up, writes the entry (source and date required), and Renzo approves before it goes in.

**From competitors:** learn the pattern, never copy the screen, branding or wording, and never claim anything against
them outside the differentiator catalogue.

**From our own deals:** after a demo, `sc-meeting-review` notes what landed. Patterns that worked on 2+ deals are
candidates to build once as a demo library module.
